# Vesper Tea · sample identity handover

Version 0.1.0. **Fictional HS108 sample project.** These files demonstrate a handover structure; they are not a real client delivery or a production-ready tea product.

## Included files

- Primary, compact and reversed SVG logo forms.
- `brand-tokens-v0.1.json` with semantic Chroma references and font roles.

## Basic use

Use the primary wordmark when horizontal room allows. Use the compact symbol in square spaces. Use the reversed form on the approved dark leaf field. Leave at least one symbol height of clear space. Do not distort, rotate, shadow or redraw the mark.

Fraunces is for short display text; DM Sans handles practical copy. Obtain fonts through the source links in the project configuration and follow their SIL Open Font License terms. Keep practical instructions readable at final size.

The palette references Chroma revision `22b9004f8042737a484c909ba7fe678b0a7e8f27`. Resolve values from that revision. Display-P3 values in Chroma are sRGB-derived, not separately authored wide-gamut colors. CMYK values are not approved; a print vendor must specify profile, stock, conversion conditions and proof before production.

## Vendor checklist

1. Confirm the intended logo form, final dimensions, substrate and output format.
2. Confirm clear space, contrast and legibility on a physical proof.
3. Verify the approved Chroma revision and font licenses.
4. Obtain production color approval before printing.
